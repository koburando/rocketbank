Enterprise Core Banking Architecture

RocketBank is built on a resilient, microservice-ready Django core integrated with PostgreSQL transactional persistence, Celery distributed tasks, and Redis low-latency caching.

Our transaction processor guarantees full ACID atomicity with row-level locks (select_for_update) to prevent race conditions during high-volume peak financial operations.

  • 🛡️ Regulatory Compliance: Licensed by the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market (ARDFM).
  • ⚡ Performance: Sub-50ms query response times via indexed IBAN lookups and precomputed aggregated selectors.
  • 🔐 Security: Granular role-based access control, PBKDF2 credential hashing, and automated login audit trails.
